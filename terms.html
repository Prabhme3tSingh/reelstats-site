What ReelStats does

ReelStats looks up the publicly visible view and like counts of public Instagram reels and TikTok videos that you link to in your spreadsheet, and writes them into your sheet. The counts are retrieved with the help of a third-party web data provider.

Free service and limits

ReelStats is free. To keep it free for everyone, each person can check a limited number of videos per day, and there is a shared monthly limit. We may change these limits, or pause or discontinue ReelStats, at any time.

Accuracy

Counts are a snapshot from the moment of each update, as publicly visible without logging in. They may differ from what Instagram or TikTok show to logged-in users, and some videos can't be looked up (private, deleted, or age-restricted).
