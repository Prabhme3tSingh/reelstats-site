ReelStats

Views and likes for your video links, right in Google Sheets

Paste Instagram reel and TikTok video links into a sheet. ReelStats fills in each video's views and likes next to its link.

How it works

  1. Put your video links in one column, and add column headers named Views and Likes (ReelStats can add them for you).
  2. Select the links and open Extensions → ReelStats → Open ReelStats.
  3. Click Update. Views and likes appear next to each link, usually within a few minutes.

Free to use

ReelStats is free. Each person can check up to 200 videos per day, and there is a shared monthly limit for everyone. Limits reset daily and monthly (UTC).

Where the numbers come from

ReelStats looks up the publicly visible view and like counts of public videos. It does not log in to Instagram or TikTok, and it can't see private, deleted, or age-restricted videos. Those rows are marked "Unavailable". If a creator has hidden their likes, the Likes cell says "Hidden".

Counts are a snapshot from the moment you run an update and may differ slightly from what the apps show.

Your data

ReelStats only works with the spreadsheet you open it in. Your links are used only to look up their stats and are deleted from our data provider after your update finishes. We don't sell data or show ads. Read the privacy policy.
